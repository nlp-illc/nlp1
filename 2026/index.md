# Assessment

The final grade is composed of 
* 1 Exam (week 8): 80%
* 3 Practicals (weeks 1-2, 3-4 and 5-6): 15%
* 1 Blog post (week 7): 5%

We have no requirements on individual grade components. A student passes the course if their final grade on SIS is 6 or more. A resit is available for students who do not achieve a passing grade; the resit grade is then the final grade. 

**Practicals** are developed _in pairs_. Each practical spans two weeks (a tutorial is offered in the first week, whereas in the second students develop a mini project based on the tutorial). Practicals are assessed in a 10-minute oral test, with the TA, on-site in the week after the practical (e.g., practical 1 spans weeks 1-2 and is assessed in week 3, etc.). A detailed scheduled will be announced shortly after the course starts, once the groups are formed. In regular circumstances, the groups are fixed throughout the course. 




**Blog post:** this is a short write-up in the form of a _blog post_ where each team summarises their research throughout the 3 practicals. The post will be accessible from this website's blog, and it will be remain available for posterity. More details will be announced soon.
